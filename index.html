Make the product the hero.

TrueFrame Studio is a local-first video production and review workflow for product creators. It helps prepare product-focused clips, requires human review, builds a final selling cut, and can upload an approved video to TikTok as a draft for the user to finish and post.

What TrueFrame Studio does

Product Master

Uses creator-provided product references as the source of truth for visual review and production checks.

Human Review

Clips must be actively viewed and approved by the user before they can move into the final export workflow.

Final Selling Cut

Combines approved source clips into a final short-form video and checks the final asset before export.

How the workflow works

1

Prepare

The creator adds product references and prepares source clips using the local production workflow.

2

Review

The creator watches the eligible clips and approves only the clips that accurately represent the product.

3

Deliver as draft

After final review, the creator may authorize a TikTok draft upload. The user completes editing and posting inside TikTok.

Creator control and compliance safeguards

TrueFrame Studio does not automatically publish content to TikTok. The current workflow is designed around creator review and draft delivery. A TikTok upload is initiated only when the user explicitly chooses to send an approved final asset.
  • Draft-only posting workflow by default.
  • Human review required before final export.
  • Final asset checks help prevent accidentally exporting an 8-second source clip instead of the reviewed final cut.
  • Users remain responsible for product claims, intellectual-property rights, and compliance with TikTok policies and applicable law.

FAQ

Does TrueFrame auto-post?

No. The current compliance-first workflow keeps direct posting disabled and uses creator-controlled draft upload.

Who decides whether a clip passes?

The creator. Automated checks can flag issues, but the final pass decision requires human review.

Is TrueFrame affiliated with TikTok?

No. TrueFrame Studio is an independent tool. TikTok is a third-party platform with its own terms, policies, and developer requirements.
