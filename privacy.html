1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how TrueFrame Studio (“TrueFrame”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the Service or connect a supported third-party platform.

2. Information the Service may process

  • Product reference media: product images, creator-owned videos, and related project files you choose to use.
  • Project metadata: product names, workflow state, review decisions, export status, captions, and other settings you enter.
  • Technical information: local application state, browser/extension storage, file metadata, and error information needed to operate the workflow.
  • TikTok authorization information: when you connect TikTok, the Service may process user-authorized OAuth information such as an access token and account identifier required to perform the requested integration.

3. Local-first processing

The current TrueFrame production workflow is designed to process and store core project assets locally on your device whenever practical. Product media is not intentionally uploaded to a TrueFrame-operated cloud server as part of the normal local production workflow.

Local browser or extension data may remain on your device until you remove it, clear browser data, reset the browser profile, or uninstall the extension.

4. TikTok draft upload

If you explicitly connect TikTok and choose to send an approved final video, TrueFrame may transmit the final media file and the metadata required for the requested draft-upload workflow to TikTok. The current compliance-first workflow is designed for user-initiated draft delivery rather than automatic public posting.

Your use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok’s own privacy policy, terms, and developer rules.

5. Access tokens and credentials

Access tokens and other authorization credentials should be treated as sensitive information. The current browser-extension workflow may store a user-authorized TikTok access token locally so the connection can function. You should not share tokens publicly or include them in screenshots, support messages, or source code.

6. How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, preserve local project state, perform review and export checks, support user-initiated integrations, diagnose errors, and improve reliability and compliance.

7. Data sale and advertising

TrueFrame Studio does not sell personal data to advertisers. The Service is not designed to share private project data with advertisers for targeted advertising.

8. Third parties

Third-party services may receive information only when you choose to use a feature that requires them. For example, TikTok receives the data necessary to complete a TikTok integration that you authorize. Experimental or optional third-party integrations, if enabled separately, may be subject to their own privacy terms.

9. Retention and deletion

Local project data generally remains until you delete it or clear/uninstall the local application data. Removing the browser extension may delete local project state. Export important files before removing the extension.

You can revoke a third-party authorization through the relevant third-party account or developer controls. Data already transferred to a third party is governed by that third party’s retention rules.

10. Security

We use a least-privilege approach in product design and request only the third-party permissions needed for enabled features. No software can guarantee absolute security, so you should protect your device, browser profile, credentials, and access tokens.

11. Children

The Service is not directed to children below the minimum age required by applicable law or by the connected platform’s terms.

12. Your choices

  • Do not connect TikTok if you do not want to authorize the integration.
  • Revoke third-party access through the applicable platform when you no longer want the connection.
  • Delete local project data or uninstall the application when you no longer want it stored on your device.
  • Review every final video and metadata package before sending it to a third-party platform.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Service, integrations, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are published.

14. Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact the developer using the support contact information associated with the official TrueFrame Studio developer listing. A public support email may be added to this page when one is designated.
